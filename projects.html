§ Plate 03 · Build artifact Model · 2025

Pollinator AI — YOLOv8 detection model

A trained YOLOv8 object-detection model and the accompanying camera-trap → inference field protocol, built for pollinator monitoring at UCSB's North Campus Open Space restoration site. The deliverable is the trained weights, a labeled training set, and a documented pipeline from raw video through detected visitation events. The scientific framing — what the model was used to ask, and what it found — lives on Research.

§ Plate 04 · Group deliverable PDF · 77 pp · 2024

Whitewater River Subbasin — Preliminary Watershed Management Plan

A 77-page preliminary watershed management plan for the Whitewater River Subbasin, produced with two groupmates for ESM 224 at the UC Santa Barbara Bren School. Covers land use, hydrology, water-quality stressors, and a layered set of long-term conservation strategies, with policy and science integrated throughout. The writing-craft lens on this document lives on Writing.

§ Plate 05 · Live application R · Shiny

California Water Quality Dashboard

An interactive R Shiny application built on NOAA CalCOFI oceanographic survey data, visualizing California coastal water-quality trends, marine chemistry, and environmental indicators across decades of monitoring. Designed for exploratory use by anyone curious about the long arc of California coastal monitoring, not just specialists.
